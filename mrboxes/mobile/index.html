mrBoxes goes Mobile

Two thumbs. One curious robot.

LEFT THUMB

Camera Control

Left / right: orbit around the robot.

Up / down: raise / lower the view.

Release: hold the camera angle.

RIGHT THUMB

Motion Control

Direction: turn and walk through 360°.

Push further: walk faster. Behind: pivot.

Release: stop and settle. Next push starts from the robot’s facing.

Landscape works best. Entering a scene requests fullscreen where supported.
