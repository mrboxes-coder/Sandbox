A ROBOT MOTION PLAYGROUND

mrBoxes goes Mobile

Two thumbs. One curious robot.
Explore the Playground, then refine the movement in Motion Review.

LEFT THUMB

Look around

Left / right: orbit the camera around the robot.

Up / down: raise or lower the view within a comfortable limit. Release to hold the camera angle.

RIGHT THUMB

Choose your direction

Push in any direction: the robot turns towards that heading. Push further for more speed; stay near the centre to stop.

Push behind: slow, pivot, then walk forward. Release to stop and settle. Each new gesture takes the robot’s current facing as forward.

Landscape works best. Entering a scene requests fullscreen where supported.
