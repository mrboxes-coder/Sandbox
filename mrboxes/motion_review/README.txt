REVIEW CONTROLS
Play/Pause, Replay, forward/backward frame buttons (1/60 second).
Timeline: drag to inspect any frame; scrubbing pauses playback.
Speed: quarter, half or normal speed. Replay and looping use fixed simulation steps.
Sequence: standard, earlier or later left-turn request.
Camera: front, side or three-quarter; drag the scene to orbit.
Space: pause/play. Left/right arrow: step back/forward (outside form fields).

MARKERS
Green ring: actual foot geometry is at ground contact (within 8mm).
Amber ring: foot is airborne.
Blue: pelvis projection onto the floor.
Pale marker: weighted support target, an animation guide, not physical centre of mass.

FOCUS
A continuous alternating contact sequence handles walk / left turn / walk.
Steering requests wait for a step boundary. Weight shifts before lift-off.
Body and arm springs retain their state; motion is not reset on steering changes.
This is an experimental visual review, not a replacement for the accepted playground.

The three repeatable sequences pass checks for fixed-step replay, alternating
feet, planted-foot position and height, and bounded pelvis pose changes.
Visual timing, weight and overlap still need artistic review.
Turn direction: choose Left turn or Right turn. Both share the refined transition, gaze and settling logic; right turns mirror the stepping and supporting legs.
Study menu: approved single turns; mixed walk/left/walk/right/walk/partial-left/walk/settle; early, halfway and late interruption; turn-to-settle with all keys released. The key display and study note show the exact programmed input and release frame. Mixed sequences last 19 seconds. Existing approved preset frame numbers are unchanged.
