Touch controller guide

Left stick: move in the direction you face.
Right stick left/right: turn 30°.
Right stick up/down: fly up/down.
A: reset position · B: leave VR.

Release the sticks to stop. No wall collision or teleportation in this first prototype. Use the headset’s boundary and begin in a clear space.
